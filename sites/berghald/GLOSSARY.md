# Berghald

Berghald is an Icelandic landscaping company. These are the terms its site and its Vefstjórn content are built from.

## Language

**Project** (UI: _Verkefni_):
A finished job Berghald shows on the site, with its photos and videos. A project belongs to one or more services.
_Avoid_: Case, job, work

**Service** (UI: _Þjónusta_):
Something Berghald offers, such as paving or tree felling. Services are also how projects are filtered.
_Avoid_: Category, tag

**Season** (UI: _Árstíð_: Vetur, Vor, Sumar, Haust):
A part of the year when a service is in demand. Services in the current season are listed first, unless they run all year.
_Avoid_: Period

**Cover** (UI: _Forsíðumynd_, the star in the gallery):
The gallery image that represents a project on cards and in its share image. For a video, its poster image.
_Avoid_: Thumbnail, main image

**Question set** (UI: _Spurningar á tilboðsformi_):
The extra questions the quote form asks for a service's kind of job, such as area in m² or number of trees.
_Avoid_: Form template

**Review** (UI: _Umsögn_):
A customer's quote shown on the home page, with their name and place.
_Avoid_: Testimonial

**Hero scene**:
The 3D animation at the top of the home page: the crane truck backs into a garden, builds a stone wall and drives away. Where it can't play, a still frame of its final moment, the **hero poster**, is shown instead.
_Avoid_: 3D hero, animation, hero image

**Home-page project** (UI: _Sýna á forsíðu_):
A published project chosen for "Nýleg verkefni" on the home page. Only the first four in order are shown.
_Avoid_: Featured project
